Red String – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Red String is a two-player co-op game made by Ishaan Arora. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

What we collect

Nothing. Red String has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store or sell any personal information.

Game Center

Online play uses Apple Game Center. Apple handles your sign-in, your Game Center name and matchmaking under Apple's Privacy Policy. Room names and passwords are turned into a matchmaking code on your device and are never sent to us.

Gameplay data

While you play together, the two phones exchange game data (positions, button presses) through Game Center so both players see the same game. This data is not stored and is not sent to us.

Saved progress

Your progress and settings are stored on your device.

Purchases

The optional unlock for worlds 3–5 is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Children

Red String does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
